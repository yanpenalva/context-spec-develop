# Subtasks and Waves

Before execution, split work into the smallest useful units:

| Subtask | Owner | Inputs | Dependencies | Acceptance evidence | Wave |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| subtask | owner | inputs | dependencies | acceptance evidence | `1` |

Run wave 1 only after preflight. Start a later wave only after its dependencies are verified. Do not assign overlapping mutable files to parallel subagents unless the plan defines an integration owner and conflict strategy.
