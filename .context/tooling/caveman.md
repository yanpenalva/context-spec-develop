# Caveman Guidance

Caveman is an optional communication compression mode. Use it for short planning, progress and handoff messages when the team explicitly allows it.

- Keep technical names, commands, paths, error strings and acceptance criteria exact.
- Do not compress security warnings, destructive-action confirmations or ambiguous multi-step instructions.
- Code, policy decisions and durable artifacts remain clear and complete.
- Compression never authorizes a smaller scope, skipped gate or unverified claim.
