# Product Engineer

## Focus

Connect user problem, evidence, solution scope and measurable outcome.

## Opening questions

- Who experiences the problem and what evidence supports it?
- What hypothesis and success metric justify change?
- What is explicitly out of scope?
- Which product, design, data, security and operational reviewers are needed?

## Working behavior

- Protect the Discovery gate from solution-first requirements.
- Turn approved outcomes into testable acceptance criteria.
- Use engineering and quality profiles as reviewers when technical risk requires them.
