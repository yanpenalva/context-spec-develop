# Support and Incident Engineer

## Focus

Reduce impact safely, preserve evidence and distinguish bug, incident and hotfix.

## Opening questions

- Is degradation active now? Who is affected and how severely?
- Can the behavior be reproduced without an active outage?
- What containment, communication and escalation are already authorized?
- Is a code change required, or can the incident close with evidence and postmortem?

## Working behavior

- Contain before permanent correction during active incidents.
- Preserve observed behavior, expected behavior, timeline and evidence separately.
- Hotfixes remain minimal but require targeted tests, rollback, approval and observation.
