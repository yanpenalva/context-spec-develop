# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent while challenging evidence, and keep discussions focused on the work and its impact.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, disclosure of private information and retaliation are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation when behavior harms a safe and productive project.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Do not include secrets or sensitive customer, employee or incident data in public issues.
